What an investor needs to know

Gas supplyBaltic Pipe (up to 10 bcm/yr), Świnoujście LNG (8.3 bcm after expansion) and a planned Gdańsk FSRU (6.1 bcm) give diversified supply. Orlen dominates imports. The EU Russian gas import ban phases in from 2026. Hormuz disruption has pushed TTF up about 150% year on year, with EU storage near 73%. A 10 MWe engine burns roughly 2,300 m³/h at 43% efficiency.Gas PLN 235/MWh delivered (2027 forward plus network and supply costs), growing 2% a year
Power pricesTGE day-ahead averaged PLN 584/MWh in August 2026 and the 2027 baseload forward PLN 496/MWh. Evening peaks run well above base and midday solar pushes prices below zero. At current gas and carbon, clean spark spreads in baseload hours are negative, so engines earn only in scarcity hours.Engine runs 1,200 h/yr at an average PLN 900/MWh; margin cannot go below zero
Trading and balancingTrade via TGE (day-ahead, intraday, forwards) or OTC, through a balancing responsible party. PSE runs central dispatch; the reformed balancing market started in June 2024, Poland joined PICASSO (aFRR) in July 2025 and is preparing for MARI (mFRR). PSE buys about 400–500 MW of aFRR and 197 MW of FCR. Sites of 5–10 MW on distribution grids will probably need aggregation to reach balancing products.Other flexibility revenue PLN 60/kW/yr (assumption)
Grid connectionThe Energy Law amendment in force from 30 April 2026 adds a non-refundable application fee of PLN 1/kW (cap PLN 100,000), raises the advance payment to PLN 60/kW, requires collateral above 1 kV, cuts validity of connection conditions to 12 months, adds milestones with automatic expiry, and introduces flexible connection agreements. Indicative timeline (our estimate, confirm with the DSO): 6–12 months for conditions, 8–16 months for equipment and build, 24–36 months to commercial operation.Operations start three years out; capex includes grid and gas connection
OwnershipPoland's screening regime applies to acquisitions of at least 20% in protected entities by investors from outside the EU, EEA and OECD. The UK is in the OECD, so a UK parent is generally outside it, although protected sectors and indirect holdings should be checked. This corrects my earlier screen, which implied UK buyers could face screening.100% ownership, no partner needed
TaxCorporate tax 19%. Dividend withholding tax is 19% domestically and reduced under the UK treaty (confirm the rate). Interest deductions are capped at PLN 3m or 30% of tax EBITDA. Losses carry forward five years.19% tax, 15-year straight-line depreciation
